Hi there!
I'm an Assistant Professor of Linguistics at the University of Pittsburgh.
As a researcher and a teacher, I focus on **computational sociolinguistics**: I study how linguistic differences like the distinction between _working_ and _workin'_ reflect social processes (**sociolinguistics**), and I do so through computational methods like machine learning.
To me, this is a natural combination.
Sociolinguistics is the study of how language and society intersect, which is obviously pretty complex, while computers are great at finding patterns in complexity (not to mention at performing repetitive tasks without getting bored).
I also work to make linguistics more inclusive and equitable.

On this site, you can learn more about my scholarly research---the [broad themes and questions](research) my research is organized around, my [current projects](curr-projects), and my [lab](lab)---and download papers from my [CV](cv).
You can also read about my [teaching](teaching) and about [me](about-me).
Finally, you can even view the [source code for this website](https://github.com/djvill/djvill.github.io).

Feel free to reach out!
